# MINCO Benchmark Calibration Strategy

MINCO currently uses the bundled Meridian synthetic reference case. Level 2 validates internal coherence, Monte Carlo behavior, optimization sensitivity, predictive baselines, and evidence integrity. It does not claim calibration to a real hospital.

Future historical or public-benchmark calibration must provide versioned operational events with timestamps, hospital identifiers, event type, pathway/cohort, resource, quantity, and source system. Required validation targets include arrivals, occupancy, transition or length-of-stay behavior, transfer flow, waiting time, and utilization.

External calibration is a prerequisite for any real-hospital deployment claim. Clinical diagnosis and treatment decisions remain outside MINCO's scope.
