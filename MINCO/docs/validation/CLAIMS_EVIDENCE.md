## Legacy baseline unsafe-excess and utilization claims

The old repository stored the following synthetic results:

- unsafe excess: 7.59 under no control vs. 4.41 under optimized network (41.9% relative reduction);
- maximum utilization: 1.166 vs. 1.037 (11.1% relative reduction).

**Finalization status: LEGACY PRE-TRANSFER-CAPACITY EVIDENCE.**

These runs predate the explicit `transfer_capacity` parameter and lane-capacity constraint. They are preserved for engineering history but are **not current V1.0 benchmark claims**. Rebaseline under the corrected model before public reuse.

## Legacy regime-adjusted policy claim

The previous regime-adjusted policy had lower mean unsafe excess than nominal optimization in four of five stored scenario means, with only two scenario comparisons qualifying as statistically validated advantages under the later paired bootstrap gate.

**Finalization status: LEGACY PRE-TRANSFER-CAPACITY EVIDENCE.** Re-run before V1.0 public reporting.

## Legacy scalability claim

The stored 200-hospital LP experiment reported 49 ms of Gurobi solver time, 59 ms wall-clock solve time, and 826 ms model-build time.

**Finalization status: HISTORICAL IMPLEMENTATION BENCHMARK.** It describes the older LP formulation and must not be presented as the final V1.0 model benchmark without a rerun.

## Superseded predictive-model governance

The old WP3 forecast dataset created `time_index` by ordering independent Monte Carlo replications and built next-step targets by shifting those replication rows. Those models remain legitimate supervised-learning research experiments, but the replication order is not an operational chronological time axis.

**Finalization status: SUPERSEDED FOR OPERATIONAL FORECAST CLAIMS.**

The current operational forecast is `src/ai/demand_forecast_quantile.py`, validated on a generated calendar-time synthetic historical replay.

## Prohibited claims at the current evidence level

Do not claim:

- real-hospital effectiveness or predictive accuracy;
- clinical safety or clinical decision support;
- realized savings or realized service-level improvement;
- literature calibration without cited parameter evidence;
- a real-time or patient-level discrete-event digital twin;
- formal robust optimization for the legacy uncertainty-adjusted formulation;
- autonomous decision making;
- production deployment readiness;
- a general-purpose AI Copilot.
