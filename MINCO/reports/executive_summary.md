# MINCO Executive Summary

MINCO is a healthcare operations decision-intelligence platform under staged engineering development. Its preserved analytical core forecasts patient-state demand, evaluates capacity pressure, solves network allocation decisions, tests policies under seeded stochastic variation, and communicates operational risk through explanations and auditable records.

In the stored synthetic baseline experiment, optimized network control reduced mean unsafe excess by 41.9% and mean maximum utilization by 11.1% relative to no control. These results are evidence-backed for the synthetic reference case only. MINCO has not yet been validated on real hospital data or deployed as a production healthcare system.
