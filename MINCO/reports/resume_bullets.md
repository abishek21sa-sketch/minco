# Evidence-Safe MINCO Resume Bullets

- Engineered MINCO, a healthcare operations decision-intelligence prototype combining Markov patient-flow forecasting, seeded Monte Carlo simulation, and Gurobi network optimization for surge, elective-admission, and ICU-transfer decisions across a synthetic multi-hospital network.
- Reduced unsafe excess, defined as demand beyond modeled safe operating capacity, by 41.9% relative to no control in the stored synthetic baseline experiment with 50 replications; also reduced mean maximum utilization by 11.1%.
- Built scenario, sensitivity, explanation, alert, API, dashboard, and audit components; then conducted an evidence-based repository audit and added data-contract validation, automated regression tests, a solver-independent smoke pipeline, CI, and reproducibility governance.
